Make `/app/flowkit/src/main.zig`, a stub now, into a tool that reads the case and every export packet from the flow collector's archive and prints one line of JSON on standard output: what left the site and which inside hosts the suspect reached. The archive runs on this box at `ARCHIVE_HOST` and `ARCHIVE_PORT` from the environment. It holds every export packet both routers sent it during the window, with the time each one arrived, plus the case line naming the suspect host, the window and the site network. `/app/flowkit/SPEC.md` defines the archive protocol, the packet layout, the facts known about the two routers and the collector, and the report layout.

The two routers disagree. One of them samples, one keeps its own clock, one was power cycled part way through, and the collector dropped some packets and later re-sent part of its spool after a restart. The spec states each of these as fact; the stub handles none of them.

The tool must be Zig, like the rest of the toolkit. The grader compiles the .zig files you leave under src the way the spec says, from main.zig, and runs the result from `/app/flowkit` three times: against the archive here on another address and port, against an archive you haven't seen with a different network, window, sampling rate and clock error, and against a small one reached by host name. It follows the page tokens the archive hands out, so anything copied out of this archive into the source is no use there. Every host row, every lateral row and both lost counts have to match exactly; one wrong number and the run fails.
